## Persona 1 — Amara, 22 · International Student

**Tab label:** Just started uni  
**Eyebrow chip:** International student · Week 1

### Headline
> Everything is new. Even the basics.

### Context
Amara moved from overseas for her degree. She has five days before her first lecture. She doesn't know what Medicare is, where the nearest bulk-billing GP is, or whether her prescription medication is available here.

### Quote
> "I didn't know I could see a doctor for free until week three. No one told me."

### Her three immediate concerns
- Finding a GP before she gets sick — and not paying $100+ for it
- Getting home from the city after 9pm safely
- Meeting people who aren't in her course group chat

### Data
> 62% of international students say the first month is the highest-stress period of their degree.  
> *Monash International Student Health Survey 2023*

3× more likely to delay healthcare due to cost uncertainty than domestic students.

### CTA
→ **Finding a GP before you need one** *(Health & Wellbeing · Week 1)*

### Tone note
She's not fragile. She's capable and prepared — but the systems here assume knowledge she doesn't have yet. Don't pity. Recognise the gap.

## Persona 2 — Liam, 25 · Domestic Graduate Mover

**Tab label:** First job in the city  
**Eyebrow chip:** New to Melbourne · Month 1

### Headline
> You know how to be independent. Just not here.

### Context
Liam moved from regional Victoria for his first full-time role. He's done independent living before — but Melbourne has its own systems, costs, and rhythms. His social network is three hours away. He's starting from scratch.

### Quote
> "I knew how to be independent. I didn't know how to be independent *here*."

### His three immediate concerns
- Getting a Myki sorted and understanding which zones he actually needs
- Figuring out what rent, bills, and groceries actually cost on a grad salary
- Building a social life when everyone already has their people

### Data
> 71% of young adults who relocate to Melbourne for work wished they had a single resource to orient themselves.  
> *ABS Internal Migration 2022–23*

Transport navigation and housing admin are the top two stressors in the first 90 days of relocation.

### CTA
→ **Getting around Melbourne** *(Getting Around · Week 1)*

### Tone note
He's not lost — he's competent in an unfamiliar context. The framing is about local knowledge, not capability. Don't infantilise.
