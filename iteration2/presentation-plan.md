# Minuri Project Presentation Plan

## Presentation Goal
- Show how Minuri supports newcomers from first awareness to practical action.
- Demonstrate two public services (`Guides` and `Near Me`) with distinct value.
- End with `Journey` as the personalized layer that combines both services.

## Suggested Run of Show (10-12 mins)
- Part 1: Landing page (2 mins)
- Part 2: Guides (3 mins)
- Part 3: Near Me (4 mins)
- Part 4: Journey (2-3 mins)

## Main Points Cheat Sheet

### Landing Page
- Start with the problem: newcomers face uncertainty and information overload.
- Position Minuri clearly: trusted guidance plus practical local actions.
- Point to the two public services: `Guides` and `Near Me`.
- Set up the transition: this is where awareness turns into action.

### Guides
- Emphasize structure and trust: practical, topic-based support.
- Show Guides as "what to do" and "how to decide."
- Highlight topic coverage across daily newcomer needs.
- Bridge to `Near Me`: from advice to nearby places and services.

### Near Me
- Emphasize action: real places users can go to now.
- Show topic-specific experiences (food, transport, health, services, social).
- Highlight key interactions: map/list sync, popups, area re-search, clear CTAs.
- Reinforce continuity: users can save places for ongoing use.

### Journey
- Position Journey as personalization, not just another page.
- Explain that it combines behavior from Guides + Near Me.
- Show a simple user story: read guide -> discover nearby options -> save and continue.
- Close with impact: Minuri supports users over time, not only in one session.

## Detailed Main Points (What Audience Sees)

### Landing Page - What to call out
- In the hero section, the audience immediately sees the newcomer problem and Minuri value in plain language.
- As you scroll, the page naturally moves from awareness to "what Minuri offers" without feeling technical.
- The two public services are visible early: `Guides` for trusted advice and `Near Me` for local action.
- The journey prompt/sidebar shows that users can start personalizing with suburb, life moment, and focus topics.
- Saved context (like saved places) hints that Minuri remembers useful actions and supports return visits.
- End this section by reinforcing that landing is not just marketing; it is a practical starting hub.

### Guides - What to call out
- The guide library appears structured by life moments and topics, not a random article feed.
- Topic filters make it clear users can focus only on what matters right now.
- Guide cards feel approachable and actionable, helping users pick a next step quickly.
- Inside a guide, content flows from "your situation" to "what it feels like" to "what to do next."
- Reading progress and bookmark behavior show that users can continue where they left off.
- The key bridge moment: each guide can directly send users to relevant nearby places in `Near Me`.

### Near Me - What to call out
- Users first see clear context: selected suburb, active topic, and focused subtype options.
- The list/map split creates a practical local discovery experience instead of a plain search result page.
- Cards/rows look different by topic, so each service type feels purpose-built (food, transit, health, admin, social).
- Map markers are easy to scan and topic-colored, with popups that show useful actions fast.
- Interactions feel connected: hovering/selecting in list and map keeps users oriented.
- "Search this area" and user-location behavior show real-world usefulness while navigating the map.
- Health view visibly prioritizes safety with crisis support and practical call-first cues.
- The save/heart action demonstrates continuity from one search into longer-term personal use.

### Journey - What to call out
- Journey onboarding asks for user story, suburb, and priorities, making personalization explicit.
- Users can choose preset moments or write their own, so the experience is both guided and flexible.
- The resulting week plan turns big uncertainty into clear daily steps.
- Day-by-day tabs and overview make progress tangible and reduce overwhelm.
- Each day combines guidance and local services, proving Guides + Near Me work as one system.
- The journey feels reusable, not one-off, with bookmarks/progress and restart options for changing needs.

## Ready-to-Speak Pitches

### Landing Page Pitch
"Imagine arriving in Melbourne and feeling that quiet panic: so many things to figure out, and no obvious place to start. Food, transport, healthcare, paperwork, community. Each task sounds small, but together they feel overwhelming.

Minuri’s landing page is designed for that exact moment.

In the hero section, we begin with reassurance and clarity. We acknowledge the stress without dramatizing it, and we make one promise: you can take this one step at a time.

As users continue down the page, the experience opens up in a deliberate flow. They see what Minuri offers, why it matters, and how it supports real life. The services area introduces two practical paths: Guides for trusted, step-by-step understanding, and Near Me for immediate local action. This is not abstract advice. It is support users can apply today.

Then the spotlight and care-focused sections deepen the emotional connection. They show that Minuri is not only functional, but also humane. We are not just listing resources; we are helping people feel seen, supported, and less alone while they settle.

The access-oriented part of the page reinforces fairness and usability: help should be easy to reach, easy to understand, and available when people need it most. That reflects a core Minuri value: support should reduce friction, not add to it.

And importantly, the landing experience includes a personal hub pattern where users can set suburb, choose their current life moment, and pick focus topics. That small act of personalization turns the page from a static homepage into a meaningful starting point. It tells users: this support can adapt to you.

So the landing page is much more than a first impression. It is the beginning of trust. It moves users from uncertainty to orientation, from orientation to action, and from action to confidence."

### Journey Pitch
"If the landing page is the welcome, Journey is Minuri’s promise in action.

Journey turns support into something personal, practical, and continuous. It listens to a user’s moment, suburb, and priorities, then translates that into a clear week-by-week path.

What users feel here is relief: instead of carrying everything at once, they can focus on one meaningful next step each day. That is Minuri’s value of clarity under pressure.

Journey also reflects trust and respect. Guidance is tailored to real life, local options are connected at the right time, and personal context is treated with care.

In the end, Journey is about belonging with confidence — helping people move from 'I’m still figuring this out' to 'I know where I’m going next, and I can do this.'"

## Part 1 - Landing Page

### Purpose
Raise awareness of newcomer challenges and quickly explain how Minuri helps.

### Key Message to Say
"Minuri gives people both trusted guidance and nearby actions, then personalizes the path over time."

### What to Demo
- Open the homepage and show the core value proposition.
- Highlight how the page frames Minuri as practical support, not just information.
- Show entry points to the two public services: `Guides` and `Near Me`.
- If available, briefly show saved/place-based context in the landing experience to hint personalization.

### Why It Matters
- Sets context for the audience before feature details.
- Establishes the problem-solution narrative in under 2 minutes.

---

## Part 2 - Guides (Public Service #1)

### Purpose
Show structured, trustworthy guidance for common newcomer scenarios.

### Key Message to Say
"Guides turn overwhelming life tasks into clear, local, step-by-step support."

### What to Demo
- Open the Guides area and show topic structure:
  - Food & Eating
  - Getting Around
  - Health & Wellbeing
  - Home & Admin
  - Social & Belonging
- Open 1-2 guide examples and emphasize practical tone and actionable advice.
- Show the bridge from a guide to `Near Me` using topic-based deep linking (came-from-guide context).

### Why It Matters
- Proves Minuri is not generic content; it is targeted and actionable.
- Shows continuity: reading guidance naturally leads to real-world action.

---

## Part 3 - Near Me (Public Service #2)

### Purpose
Show location-aware discovery with a richer, map-first/list-first experience by topic.

### Key Message to Say
"Near Me converts guidance into immediate local options people can use right now."

### What to Demo
- Start on one topic (e.g., `Food & Eating`) and show:
  - Topic tabs and subtypes
  - Updated cards with photos, ratings/meta, status, and clear CTAs
  - Directions/call/website actions
- Switch topics to show unique UI behavior:
  - `Getting Around`: compact transit-focused rows + journey planning action
  - `Health & Wellbeing`: health-specific nudges and crisis support visibility
  - `Home & Admin`: service-oriented actions (especially website-first)
  - `Social & Belonging`: discovery experience without ranking emphasis
- Show map interactions:
  - Marker/list hover linking
  - Popup details
  - Marker colors by topic
  - "Search this area"
  - User-location indicator and distance-based relevance (if location enabled)
- Save a place (heart) to show continuity into the broader Minuri experience.

### Why It Matters
- Demonstrates "information -> action" with concrete, local outcomes.
- Shows the product feels dynamic and personalized even before full Journey mode.

---

## Part 4 - Journey (Personalized Experience)

### Purpose
Present Journey as the personalized layer that combines Guides + Near Me into one ongoing support flow.

### Key Message to Say
"Journey is where Minuri becomes personal: it learns what matters to you and connects advice with nearby action over time."

### What to Demo
- Explain Journey as a user-specific pathway rather than a one-time search.
- Show how user actions from public services feed Journey:
  - Guide topics viewed
  - Places saved or revisited from Near Me
  - Preferences and recurring needs
- Walk through an example:
  - User reads a guide about healthcare
  - Jumps to nearby clinics/support services
  - Saves options and gets a clearer next-step path in Journey
- Position Journey as the product’s long-term retention and impact layer.

### Why It Matters
- Ties the whole product narrative together.
- Shows Minuri can scale from first-time visitors to returning users with evolving needs.
